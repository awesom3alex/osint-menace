Shareable report redaction

Enter the exact names, emails, phone numbers, or other text to hide, one per line. Matching is case insensitive and replaces matching text throughout PDF, Word, Markdown, and HTML report content. Include alternate spellings and phone formats separately.

All graph images, photos, and screenshots are omitted while redaction is enabled because they may contain visible identifiers. Graph descriptions and connections remain available as redacted text. Original case records, JSON exports, backups, and the investigation workspace retain the original information.
